## **🎤 Interview Prep Strategy**

### **For IT Security Engineer, Focus On:**

**Technical Demonstrations You Should Prepare:**

1. **SIEM Experience**: Be ready to discuss Splunk/Elastic/Sentinel implementations
1. **Defense in Depth**: Explain how you’d implement preventive, detective, AND reactive controls
1. **Cloud Security**: AWS or Azure security configurations (IAM, security groups, etc.)
1. **Vulnerability Management**: Patch management workflows
1. **ISO27001**: Basic understanding of controls and implementation

**Topics They’ll Likely Ask About:**

- “How would you secure a new cloud environment?”
- “Describe your experience with SIEM tools”
- “Walk me through your vulnerability management process”
- “How do you implement Defense in Depth?”
- “Tell me about a time you hardened a system”

**Dutch Context Note:** If the Team is Dutch, be prepared to discuss:

- NIS2 Directive (EU cybersecurity directive)
- GDPR compliance
- Working with Dutch clients
