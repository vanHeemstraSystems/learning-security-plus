## **📚 Study & Retention Strategy (Apply Daily)**

### **Before Each Session:**

1. **5-minute review**: Flashcards from previous day
1. **Brain dump prep**: Keep paper ready for lists/acronyms you’re memorizing

### **During Each Session:**

1. **Active note-taking**: Write summaries in YOUR OWN WORDS (don’t copy verbatim)
1. **Say it out loud**: Verbalize key concepts as you learn them
1. **Create connections**: Link each topic to the bigger security picture

### **After Each Session:**

1. **Immediate flashcard creation**: Make Anki cards for:

- Every acronym (what it stands for + what it does)
- Key processes and their steps
- Port numbers and protocols
- Any concept you found confusing

1. **Teach it**: Explain one concept to someone (or pretend to)
1. **Mini practice quiz**: 5-10 questions from that section

### **Weekly:**

1. **Review ALL flashcards** - even on non-study days
1. **Practice exam** - track weak areas
1. **Revisit weak topics** - rewatch those specific videos
